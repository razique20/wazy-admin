# Finavig Logo — Meaning & Design Rationale

## What the logo is

The Finavig logo (`src/components/ui/logo.tsx`) is a four-quadrant mark made of four rounded squares arranged in a 2×2 grid. Each quadrant is filled with a distinct colour, and the four shapes fold inward so that a small square void sits at the very centre of the mark. The same mark is exposed two ways in the codebase:

- **`FinavigLogo`** — the raw SVG used directly in headers, the login page, and the app shell.
- **`FinavigBrand`** — the full wordmark: the mark plus the "finavig" wordmark, an "Admin" pill, and the "Financial Navigator" tagline.

## The four colours

| Quadrant | Colour | Hex |
|----------|--------|-----|
| Top-left | Green | `#107C41` |
| Top-right | Red | `#E81123` |
| Bottom-left | Blue | `#0078D4` |
| Bottom-right | Yellow | `#FFB900` |

The four colours are evenly distributed across the grid (one per quadrant) so the mark reads as **complete, balanced, and structured** — not as a single hue with a decoration on it. That balance mirrors the platform itself: a console that brings together several parts of the business (collections, documents, finance, subscriptions, system administration) into one coherent view.

## The inner cutout — the navigational core

The four quadrant paths are not simple rectangles that meet edge to edge. Each one tucks inward at the centre, leaving a small empty square in the middle of the mark. That void is the whole point of the design:

- It reads as a **window, portal, or compass point** — a space you move into and through.
- It is the visual hook for the product name: **Finavig = Financial Navigator**. The logo literally centres on a navigational void.
- The `FinavigBrand` wordmark reinforces it with the tagline **"Financial Navigator"** sitting right beneath the mark.

So the logo is not just four coloured blocks. It is four coloured quadrants arranged **around** a centre — the centre is where the navigation happens.

## Why this shape fits the platform

Finavig is an **admin console / financial intelligence and document-expiry platform**. The logo's structure maps onto that role:

- **Four quadrants** — the console unifies several concern areas (document collections & expiries, finance & expense ledger, subscription tiers, system administration). Four quadrants suggest a complete, bounded workspace rather than a single tool.
- **Rounded, clean geometry** — the mark is friendly but still structured and precise, which fits a platform that admins rely on for document tracking and financial data, not a playful consumer brand.
- **Central void** — a console is a place you enter to manage operations. The empty centre of the mark acts as the "door" into that workspace, and the name "Financial Navigator" tells you what you do once you are inside.
- **Wordmark + Admin pill** — the `FinavigBrand` variant makes the owner and the role explicit: this is the **finavig** mark, and the blue "Admin" pill labels it as the administrator portal (seen on the login page's top bar).

## Where it appears

- **Login page** (`src/app/login/page.tsx`) — top bar mark + wordmark, plus the "Admin Portal" label.
- **App shell** (`src/components/layout/app-shell.tsx`) — persistent header logo.
- **Browser & mobile icons** — favicon-16/32, apple-touch-icon, android-chrome-192/512, and the transparent/full/horizontal PNG assets generated by `scripts/generate-mobile-assets.py`.

## In one sentence

Finavig's logo is a balanced four-colour quadrant mark whose shapes fold inward to leave a square void at the centre; that void is the navigational core that ties the visual directly to the name "Financial Navigator" and to the platform's role as an admin console you step into to manage documents, finance, subscriptions, and system operations.
